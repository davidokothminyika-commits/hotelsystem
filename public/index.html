The hotel

Built for staying in rather than passing through

Aurelia Grand opened in 1974 on the north bank of the river and has been run by the same family ever since. Sixty rooms, no two identical, spread over four floors with a rooftop terrace that looks back toward the skyline.

The kitchen works with four growers within a day's drive of Nairobi, which means the breakfast buffet changes through the year rather than repeating. Reception is staffed around the clock, and the team is small enough that most of them have been here a decade.

Dining

The kitchen follows the season, not a fixed menu

The restaurant serves breakfast from six and dinner until ten, with a short lunch menu for business guests. The menu changes when the produce does, which means the card is rewritten most weeks.

Guests in house can order from the same menu through room service, delivered to the room within forty minutes.
